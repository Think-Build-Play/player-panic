Player Panic - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Player Panic has no accounts, no advertising, and no tracking. The sections below describe the limited technical information involved in running the app.

Purchases

The optional Launch Deck Pack is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

App updates

Each time the app opens, it checks Expo's update service for a newer version. The check sends basic technical details needed to answer it - your operating system, the app version, our project ID, and a random identifier for this installation of the app - to Expo's servers. How Expo handles this data: https://expo.dev/privacy-explained Expo also combines these checks into app usage statistics (how often the app is used over time, and on which platforms), which Expo makes visible to us in its developer dashboard.

On-device storage

The app stores your unlock state and game settings on your device only. Apple keeps its own record of your purchase, which is what lets you restore it on a new device.

Diagnostics

If you choose to share diagnostics with Apple, Apple may share crash reports with app developers.

Children

The app has no accounts and does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information.
