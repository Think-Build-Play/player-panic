Common questions

1) Restore your purchase: your Launch Deck Pack is linked to the Apple Account you bought it with. After reinstalling the app or changing iPhone, the app is designed to check for your purchase when it opens and unlock by itself. If decks still show locked, tap any locked deck, then tap "Restore purchase". The iPhone must be signed in to the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. Restoring a purchase you already own does not charge you again.

2) Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

3) The purchase screen shows no price: check your internet connection, fully close the app, and reopen it. The price is read live from the App Store.
